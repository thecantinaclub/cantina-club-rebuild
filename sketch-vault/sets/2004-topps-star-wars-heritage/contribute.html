Register a New Brandon Sketch Card

Help expand the archive of Brandon's original sketch cards from the 2004 Topps Star Wars Heritage set. Provide the details you know and upload photographs of the card.

This is a draft questionnaire. It does not send or save submissions yet. Once the submission system is connected, your entry will remain private until an administrator reviews and approves it.

1. Set and artist

This questionnaire is for a new sketch card by Brandon in this set. If the card already appears in the gallery, use that card's own contribution options instead.

2. Sketch card information

Fill in what you know. Unknown details can be left blank. Do not create an archive number yourself.

3. Card photographs

You can select more than one photo, including the front, back, signature, and close-up details. Clear photographs of the actual card are preferred.

4. Contributor credit

When the member system is connected, signed-in members can be credited by their profile name. Guests can contribute anonymously. Email addresses will not be published.

5. Before submitting

An administrator will check the information and photographs. The card will appear in Brandon's public gallery only after approval. The administrator may correct or complete the record during review.
