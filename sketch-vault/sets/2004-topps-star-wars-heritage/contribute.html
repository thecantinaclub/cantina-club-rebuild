Contribute to the Sketch Vault

Help document original Star Wars sketch cards. You can contribute photographs, information, or both.

This is a draft form. Submissions are not sent or saved yet. The submission and admin approval connection must be completed before this page is ready for public use.

1. Contribution type

Existing cards must be matched to their gallery record to avoid duplicates. Card matching will be connected before submissions are enabled.

2. Card information

Leave unknown information blank. Do not invent an archive code. For existing cards, these fields are suggestions until approved.

3. Photographs

JPEG, PNG, and WebP images are supported by the current storage setup. The upload limit and secure upload process will be connected before launch.

4. Contributor credit

Signed-in members will be credited using their chosen profile name. Guests will be credited as Anonymous. Email addresses will never be published.

5. Review

Contributions will remain private until reviewed by an administrator. Approved photos and approved information changes can be handled separately.
