About this release

This archive will document the cards, variations, promotional items and related products associated with the 1993 Star Wars Galaxy Series 1 release.

The archive is being researched. Categories will be added after their contents have been checked.

Archive categories

Card sets and variations

Base cards, foil parallel cards, etched foil cards, holofoil cards and autograph cards will be documented here as their checklists are verified.

Detailed pages coming after research.

Promotional items and panels

Promo cards and the four known 9-card panels will be documented separately from the standard base-card checklist.
