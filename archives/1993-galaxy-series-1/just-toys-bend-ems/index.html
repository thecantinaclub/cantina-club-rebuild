Just Toys Bend-Ems

Associated with 1993 Topps Star Wars Galaxy Series 1

Overview

Just Toys produced bendable Star Wars figures known as Bend-Ems. Individual figures were packaged with lettered trading cards using artwork from Topps' Star Wars Galaxy releases. The cards were essentially reprints with letter codes instead of the original Galaxy card numbers.

The figures and their cards were released across the 1993–1995 period. Most cards depict the character associated with the figure, although some use artwork with a broader subject.

Gift packs containing four figures and four cards were also available. The Bend-Ems cards are related collectibles, not additional numbered cards in the regular 140-card Galaxy Series 1 checklist.

Figure manufacturer: Just Toys

Related card issuer: Topps

Release period: 1993–1995, as reported by collector references

Distribution: Packaged with Bend-Ems figures; four-figure gift packs were also available

TraderCracks lists a 28-card run spanning Galaxy Series 1 and Series 2, with cards Q through BB assigned to the wider run. The two lists should not be combined without further verification.

Relationship to the Galaxy base cards

Many Bend-Ems cards reproduce artwork from the regular Galaxy trading-card sets, but use lettered identifiers. The original Galaxy card number and the Bend-Ems letter are different identification systems.

For archive purposes, record the Bend-Ems letter, depicted subject, associated figure, artwork match, and any variation in the card or packaging. A matching image does not make the Bend-Ems card part of the regular 140-card Series 1 checklist.
