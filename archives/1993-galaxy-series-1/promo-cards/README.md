# 1993 Star Wars Galaxy Series 1 — Promo Cards

This section documents confirmed promotional cards associated with the 1993 Star Wars Galaxy Series 1 release.

For each item, record the card title, card number, artwork, artist, front and back details, distribution method and source used to verify the information.

**Archive note:** Only confirmed promotional cards should be included here. Keep preview cards and other special cards in their appropriate categories unless reliable evidence establishes that they are also promotional cards.
