Promo Cards

1993 Topps Star Wars Galaxy Series 1

Overview

A number of promotional cards are associated with the 1993 Topps Star Wars Galaxy Series 1 release. They were distributed through different channels, including comic-book promotions, collector publications, bookstore promotions, and related products.

These cards are documented separately from the regular 140-card base checklist, the foil parallel, the Etched Foil inserts, and the Holofoil cards supplied with the Millennium Falcon Factory Set.

Promotional cards do not all share one numbering system or one distribution method. Some have printed promotional codes, while others are unnumbered. Where two versions are known, the differences should be recorded rather than treating them as identical copies.

Promotional card groups

DH promotional cards

Several cards associated with Dark Horse comic-book publications carry the codes DH1, DH2, or DH3. Some DH1 and DH3 cards are known in two versions, distinguished by the wording or orientation of the text on the back.

Printed code Recorded identification Notes
DH1 Version 1 The final line on the back reads “December 1994”.
DH1 Version 2 The final line on the back reads “1994”.
DH2 Boba Fett Associated with Classic Star Wars: The Early Adventures #3.
DH3 Version 1 The final line on the back reads “Coming in December 1994”.
DH3 Version 2 The final line on the back reads “December 1994”.

The DH1 cards are associated with Star Wars: Dark Lords of the Sith #1, and the DH3 cards with Classic Star Wars: Return of the Jedi #1.

Unnumbered promotional cards

Published promotional-card references also document unnumbered cards featuring the following subjects:

  • Boba Fett and Dengar
  • Jabba the Hutt — including an oversized version and a standard-size version
  • Princess Leia
  • Princess Leia, a Stormtrooper, and a giant lizard — oversized format
  • A Stormtrooper and a giant lizard
  • The Truce at Bakura

These cards have different reported distribution histories, including comic-book promotions, collector magazines, and a bookstore giveaway. Their dimensions and artwork credits vary by card.

Nine-card promotional panels

Four known nine-card panels reproduce artwork associated with the regular base set. They are uncut panels rather than nine additional individual cards, and panel appearances do not increase the 140-card base checklist.
