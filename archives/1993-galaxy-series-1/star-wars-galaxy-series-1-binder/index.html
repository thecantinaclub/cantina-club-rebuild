Star Wars Galaxy Series 1 Binder

1993 Topps Star Wars Galaxy Series 1

Overview

The Star Wars Galaxy Series 1 collector's binder is a related collecting item associated with the 1993 Topps Star Wars Galaxy Series 1 release. Binder-related promotional material is documented separately from the regular trading-card checklist.

This archive section records the binder and its known associated premium card. It does not treat the binder itself as a trading card or add its contents to the 140-card base checklist.

Known associated card

SWB1 — Grand Moff Tarkin and Darth Vader

A promotional card identified by the code SWB1 depicts Grand Moff Tarkin and Darth Vader. Collector references associate it with the Star Wars Galaxy Series 1 collector's binder.

  • Issuer: Topps
  • Year: 1993
  • Printed code: SWB1
  • Card subject: Grand Moff Tarkin and Darth Vader
  • Artist: Doug Medved
  • Reported dimensions: 2½ × 3½ inches
  • Reported country of origin: United States
  • Reported distribution: Collector's binder premium

These details are recorded from collector-reference information. Physical examples and original packaging are useful for confirming the exact distribution and product contents.

Relationship to the main set

The binder is a related collecting product, not an additional card set. Its associated premium card is documented separately from the 140 numbered base cards, the foil parallel, the Etched Foil inserts, and the Holofoil cards.
