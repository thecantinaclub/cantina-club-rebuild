Millennium Falcon Factory Set

1993 Topps Star Wars Galaxy Series 1

← Back to Galaxy Series 1

The Millennium Falcon Factory Set was sold separately and brought together the Star Wars Galaxy Series 1 base cards with additional factory-set items. This page documents the product and its reported contents, rather than treating every included item as a separate base-set card.

Checklist rule: The standard Star Wars Galaxy Series 1 base set contains 140 cards. Factory-set versions, special cards, holograms and other inclusions are documented separately where appropriate.

Factory Set Contents

1. Millennium Falcon Display and Storage Box

A plastic Millennium Falcon box supplied with the factory set, serving as both the product's packaging and a display or storage container.

Product photographs and further details to be added.

2. Standard 140-Card Base Set with Foil Accents

The factory set includes the 140 base cards with foil accents added to the cards.

These factory-set versions are not counted as another 140 cards in the standard base checklist.

Exact foil treatment and card-by-card details to be documented.

3. Six Holofoil Cards

The factory set includes six holofoil cards that replace the etched foil cards found in the related release. The holofoils have a starry background and use the same card backs as the corresponding etched foil cards.

The identities and card numbers of all six holofoils still need reliable confirmation. They will not be named until verified.

4. Darth Vader Hologram

A Darth Vader hologram is listed among the factory-set contents.

Its precise format, dimensions and production details remain to be documented.

5. Card #0 — Ken Steacy

A special card associated with the factory set. Its artwork by Ken Steacy is based on the image used for base card #125.

View the Card #0 record →

6. Star Wars Galaxy II Preview Card

A Galaxy II preview card is reported as part of the factory-set contents.

View the Galaxy II Preview Card record →

Its exact identity and relationship to promotional card P4 remain unconfirmed.

7. Randomly Selected Autograph Card

The factory set is reported to include an autograph card selected at random. The specific signer and card therefore vary.

The eligible autograph checklist, insertion details and odds still need verification.

Archive Classification

  • The 140 standard cards belong to the base-set checklist.
  • Foil-accent versions are documented as factory-set versions, not extra base cards.
  • The six holofoils are a distinct category from the etched foil cards.
  • Card #0 and the Galaxy II preview card have separate special-card records.
  • The Darth Vader hologram is a separate product inclusion.
  • The randomly selected autograph is a variable inclusion, not one fixed card.

Research Status

This product record is based on the currently available contents information. Unconfirmed checklist details will be added only when supported by reliable references or clear images of the product and its contents.
