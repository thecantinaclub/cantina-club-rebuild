Holofoil Cards

1993 Topps Star Wars Galaxy Series 1

Overview

The 1993 Topps Star Wars Galaxy Series 1 Holofoil cards are a special six-card group associated with the Millennium Falcon Factory Set. They were supplied in place of the regular Etched Foil insert cards.

These are distinct from the 140 foil-enhanced parallel versions of the regular base set. The Holofoil cards have a holographic, starry-background appearance and are associated with the corresponding Etched Foil subjects.

Issuer: Topps
Year: 1993
Set size: 6 cards
Distribution: Millennium Falcon Factory Set

Distribution and identification

The Holofoil cards are associated with the Millennium Falcon Factory Set, a separately sold product that included the 140 base cards with foil accents and other special contents.

When identifying a possible Holofoil card, compare its holographic starry background and artwork with the corresponding Etched Foil card. Photographs of both the front and back are useful for documenting differences and confirming an identification.

The subject matches are recorded as a checklist cross-reference. Exact card-back wording, numbering, dimensions, and other physical details should be confirmed against examples before being treated as definitive.

The separate six-card insert set reported in retail packs at an insertion rate of one card per 18 packs.
