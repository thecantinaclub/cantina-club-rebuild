Autograph Cards

1993 Topps Star Wars Galaxy Series 1

Overview

This section documents autograph cards associated with 1993 Topps Star Wars Galaxy Series 1, including signed cards reported as part of the Millennium Falcon Factory Set.

Autograph cards are documented separately from the regular 140-card base set and from the etched-foil and holofoil inserts.

Known factory-set connection

Collector references report that the Millennium Falcon Factory Set could include an autograph card. The signer, card design, and exact details must be established from reliable checklist information and, where possible, physical examples.

An autograph card associated with the factory set should not automatically be treated as part of the 140-card base checklist.

Checklist status

A complete, verified autograph checklist has not yet been established for this archive.

As evidence is collected, each entry should record the card title or subject, signer, card number if present, artwork, distribution method, and the source supporting the identification.

Unconfirmed signers, card numbers, quantities, and distribution details will remain unconfirmed until supporting evidence is found.
