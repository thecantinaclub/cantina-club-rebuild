Autograph Cards

1993 Topps Star Wars Galaxy Series 1

Overview

The 1993 Topps Star Wars Galaxy Series 1 release included artist autograph redemption cards. Collectors who found a redemption card in a retail pack could redeem it for an autograph card featuring a randomly selected artist.

The signed cards were regular cards from the 140-card base set, signed by the artist associated with the artwork. They were not a separately numbered autograph insert set.

Retail pack redemption

  • Redemption cards were randomly inserted into retail packs.
  • The redemption reward was an artist autograph.
  • The artist was randomly selected.
  • The signed card was a base-set card signed by its featured artist.

The complete list of artists who fulfilled redemptions has not yet been established. The artists represented in the base set should not automatically be treated as a confirmed autograph checklist.

Millennium Falcon Factory Set autograph

The special Millennium Falcon Factory Set also included one random artist autograph. This was an additional factory-set feature alongside the 140 foil-stamped base cards, six holofoil cards, Darth Vader hologram, and Galaxy Series Two preview card.

Published collector references confirm the random autograph feature, but a complete list of possible signers and the exact fulfilment details still require further verification.

Checklist and variations

No separate, fixed, numbered autograph checklist has been confirmed for this release. Signed examples can be identified by the original base-card number, artwork, and the artist's signature.

Each documented example should record:

  • Base-card number and title
  • Artist who signed the card
  • Signature location and any inscriptions
  • Evidence of authenticity, if available
  • Distribution history, when known
  • Photographs of the front and back

Do not assume that every base-set artist signed cards, or that all signed examples came from the same distribution method. Individual examples and signer lists remain subject to verification.
