Overview

The 1993 Topps Star Wars Galaxy Series 1 Etched Foil insert set contains six cards featuring characters from the Star Wars universe.

These are separate inserts, not part of the 140-card base checklist and not the foil-stamped base cards supplied in the Millennium Falcon Factory Set.
