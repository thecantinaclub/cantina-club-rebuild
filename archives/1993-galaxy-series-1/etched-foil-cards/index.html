Etched Foil Cards

1993 Topps Star Wars Galaxy Series 1

Overview

The 1993 Topps Star Wars Galaxy Series 1 Etched Foil insert set contains six cards featuring characters from the Star Wars universe.

These are separate insert cards, not part of the regular 140-card base checklist and not the same as the 140 foil-stamped base cards supplied in the Millennium Falcon Factory Set.

Issuer: Topps
Year: 1993
Insert set size: 6 cards
Reported insertion rate: 1 per 18 packs

Complete checklist

The following six-card checklist is documented by published collector references.

Insert number Card subject
1 Darth Vader
2 Han Solo
3 Luke Skywalker
4 Chewbacca
5 Obi-Wan and Yoda
6 Princess Leia

Insert numbers above refer to the six-card Etched Foil checklist, not the numbers of the regular base cards.

Distribution

Published checklists report an insertion rate of one Etched Foil card per 18 packs. This is a stated pack insertion rate, not a guarantee that an individual box contains a particular card or a complete six-card set.

The six Etched Foil cards were replaced by six Holofoil cards in the special Millennium Falcon Factory Set.

Distinction from other card types

Etched Foil inserts

The six-card insert set documented on this page, reported at one card per 18 packs.

Foil parallel

A separate 140-card parallel of the regular base set, with foil enhancement. These cards were included in the Millennium Falcon Factory Set.

View the Foil Parallel Cards section →

Holofoil cards

A six-card set included in the Millennium Falcon Factory Set in place of the regular Etched Foil inserts. The checklist subjects correspond to the six Etched Foil subjects.
