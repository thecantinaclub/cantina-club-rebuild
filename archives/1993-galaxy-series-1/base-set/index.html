Star Wars Galaxy Series 1 — Base Set

1993 Topps Star Wars Galaxy Series 1

Overview

The standard Star Wars Galaxy Series 1 base set was released by Topps in 1993 and contains 140 cards featuring artwork inspired by the Star Wars universe.

This section documents the standard base cards separately from foil parallels, etched foil cards, holofoils, autograph cards, promotional items, uncut panels and special factory-set inclusions.

Base Set Checklist

Total cards: 140

The checklist will contain an individual record for each card, including its card number, title, artist, characters or subjects, and other verified details where available.
