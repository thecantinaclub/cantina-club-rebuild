About the nine-card panels

Panel appearances are cross-references to existing base cards. They are not additional base cards and do not increase the 140-card checklist total.

  • Panel 1: Cards #87, #88, #89, #97, #98, #99, #107, #108 and #109. Associated with the Art of Star Wars Galaxy book.
  • Panel 2: Cards #116, #117, #118, #126, #127, #128, #136, #137 and #138.
  • Panel 3: Cards #47, #82, #83, #91, #92, #93, #101, #102 and #103.
  • Panel 4: Cards #113, #114, #115, #123, #124, #125, #133, #134 and #135.

Panel records are documented separately in the 9-Card Panels archive. Artist credits and other details will be added as they are verified.
