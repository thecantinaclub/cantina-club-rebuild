1993 Topps Star Wars Galaxy Series 1

Base Set Checklist

This checklist covers the 140-card retail base set of 1993 Topps Star Wars Galaxy Series 1. Cards are organized by their original checklist sections.

The four known nine-card panels reproduce designs from existing base-set cards. Panel appearances are cross-references, not additional base cards, and do not increase the base-set total.

About the 9-card panels

Panel labels identify which known nine-card panel reproduces a card's design. A card may be listed on more than one panel if supported by the documented panel checklist.

  • Panel 1: Cards #87, #88, #89, #97, #98, #99, #107, #108 and #109. Associated with the Art of Star Wars Galaxy book.
  • Panel 2: Cards #116, #117, #118, #126, #127, #128, #136, #137 and #138.
  • Panel 3: Cards #47, #82, #83, #91, #92, #93, #101, #102 and #103.
  • Panel 4: Cards #113, #114, #115, #123, #124, #125, #133, #134 and #135.

The panel records belong in the separate 9-Card Panels archive. This checklist only records each base card's panel association.
